# 00 - Project Overview: EchoCrew 🌊

## Executive Summary
EchoCrew is an operational management platform designed for dispatchers, incident commanders, and field response crews. It provides real-time situational awareness, spatial deduplication of emergency reports, automated task routing, fleet vehicle monitoring, and AI-driven predictive hotspot analysis.

---

## Key Features & Capabilities
- **Incident Reporting & Spatial Deduplication**: Automatically deduplicate incoming field reports using spatial radius buffering and fuzzy text matching.
- **Dynamic Task & Crew Dispatch**: Intelligent matching of task requirements with crew capabilities and vehicle capacity.
- **Command Dashboard**: Live operational metrics, active response crews, and geospatial risk hotspot visualizations.
- **AI-Powered Predictive Hotspot Analysis**: AI models evaluating report volume trends and geographic clusters to forecast emergency hotspots.

---

## Core Objectives
1. **Response Efficiency**: Reduce mean time from report logging to crew deployment.
2. **Resource Optimization**: Maximize utility fleet capacity utilization.
3. **Data Integrity**: Eliminate duplicate incident tickets in high-density emergency situations.
